# Appendix C. Glossary

Words ano-mp uses, and what they mean there.

**Album artist**: the artist an album is filed under, from the files'
"album artist" tag. For a compilation it is usually **Various Artists**.

**Analysis** (loudness analysis): ano-mp listening to each track in the
background to measure its loudness, find silences and segues, and check it
decodes. See [chapter 3](03-playing-music.md#the-loudness-analysis).

**Bit depth** (bits per sample): how finely each sample is measured. CDs
use 16 bits; studio files often 24.

**Bit rate**: how much data a second of the file takes, in kbps. For lossy
files, higher usually sounds better.

**Buffer**: the small amount of sound prepared ahead for the device.
Smaller responds sooner; larger is safer against crackles.

**Calm mode**: a visualizer setting with no pulses on beats and slower
movement, for anyone affected by flashing light.

**Chapters**: parts of one long file, such as an audiobook's, each shown as
a track.

**Compilation**: an album with tracks by several artists, filed under
**Various Artists**.

**Crossfade**: fading one track out as the next fades in.

**Crossfeed**: blending a little of each stereo channel into the other, so
headphones sound more like speakers.

**Cue sheet**: a small text file (.cue) that says where each track of a
one-file album begins.

**Embedded picture**: a picture stored inside a music file, usually its
cover.

**Equaliser**: controls that make bands of frequencies, from deep bass to
high treble, louder or quieter.

**Favourite**: a track, album or artist you've hearted.

**Feature**: an optional part of ano-mp, turned on or off in **Settings ›
Features**.

**Folder** (library folder): a folder you added to the library. ano-mp
reads everything in it and its subfolders, and remembers that you allowed
it to.

**Gapless**: tracks running into each other with no silence between them,
as on the album.

**Hi-res**: files with a higher sample rate or bit depth than a CD's.

**Library**: everything ano-mp knows about your music: the tracks in your
folders, and your playlists, hearts, ratings and history. It is kept on
your Mac, apart from your music files.

**Locate**: telling ano-mp where a folder it can't find is now.

**Lossless**: a format that keeps the sound exactly as recorded (FLAC,
Apple Lossless, WAV, AIFF). **Lossy** formats (MP3, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, Opus)
leave out detail you are unlikely to hear, to make files smaller.

**Match**: which release on an online service an album is, or which
person an artist is. ano-mp matches automatically; you can choose another
with **Find Details…** or **Wrong artist?**.

**Mini player**: a small window with just the player bar.

**Mix** (of an effect): how much of the effect you hear against the music.

**Online sources**: the services ano-mp can look music up on: MusicBrainz,
the Cover Art Archive, Wikipedia and Discogs.

**Playback preferences**: your own rules for a track or album: skip it,
never shuffle it, make it louder or quieter, trim it.

**Player bar**: the strip along the bottom of the window with the track
playing and the controls.

**Queue**: the list of what plays, in order.

**Radio** (library radio): tracks from your library like one you chose,
added as it plays.

**Rescan**: looking through a folder again for new, changed and removed
files.

**ReplayGain**: tags in music files measuring their loudness, used to
play everything at about the same volume.

**Resampling**: converting sound from one sample rate to another.

**Sample rate**: how many times a second the sound is measured: 44.1 kHz on
CDs, 48 to 192 kHz in hi-res files.

**Scan**: reading a folder's files to add them to the library.

**Segue**: one track running into the next without a break, as on a live
album or a DJ mix.

**Signal path**: every step the sound takes from the file to your
speakers.

**Smart playlist**: a playlist that fills itself with the tracks that
match its rules.

**Tags**: the information inside a music file: title, artist, album and so
on.

**Theme**: the app's colours, font, text size, density and corners.

**Token** (API key): a code from a service (Discogs, ListenBrainz) that
lets ano-mp use your account there. Kept in the macOS keychain.

**Transcode**: a file converted from one format to another. A "lossless"
file made from an MP3 isn't really lossless; the health report lists
suspected ones.

**View**: one way of browsing the library, listed under **Library** in the
sidebar, such as **Album artist** or **Genre**.

**Visualizer**: pictures that move with the music.

**Work**: in classical music, a piece made of movements, such as a
symphony.
