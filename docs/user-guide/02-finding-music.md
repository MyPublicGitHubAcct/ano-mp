# 2. Finding music

This chapter covers every way to find something to play: the sidebar's
views, browsing the library, searching, and the pages for each artist and
album.

## The sidebar's views

### Home

**Home** (⌘1) gathers ways into your library besides browsing it. Each part
shows only while its feature is on (in **Settings › Features**) and has
something to show, in this order:

- **Beyond your library**: artists you don't have, like the ones you play
  most (needs **Recommendations from outside the library**, which goes
  online; see [chapter 6](06-online-information.md#listenbrainz)).
- **Released on this day**: albums first released on today's date in an
  earlier year (**Released on this day**). It needs release dates, which
  come from MusicBrainz or your tags.
- **Recently played**: what you played last, with plays from one album
  together (**Recently played**).
- **A year ago today**: what you were playing on this date last year.
- **Not played for a year**: favourites you haven't played for a year.
- **Added this week**, **Added this month** and **Added earlier**: the
  albums that came into the library most recently (**Recently added**).
- **Never played**: albums you haven't played yet.
- **You might like**: albums like what you play that you haven't played
  lately (**Recommendations**). Click the heading to open it.

The history parts need **Listening history** on. If every part is off,
Home says which features to turn on.

Click an album's cover to open it, or its play button to play it.
Right-click for more.

### Favourites

**Favourites** (⌘3) lists everything you've hearted: artists, albums and
tracks, newest first. **Play favourite tracks** plays the tracks. See
[chapter 4](04-playlists-and-favourites.md#favourites).

### Artists

**Artists** lists every artist in the library, including each artist of a
duet or a collaboration. Type in **Filter artists** to narrow the list
(capitals and accents don't matter). Click an artist to open their page
(below); right-click to play, shuffle, queue or heart all their tracks.

### History

**History** shows what you've played (needs **Listening history**, on at
first). A track counts as played once half of it, or four minutes of it,
has played, whichever comes first.

- **Top 20 of** a period: choose **Year** or **Month**, step with
  **Earlier** and **Later**, and choose **What to count**: **Tracks**,
  **Albums** or **Artists**. **Play these** plays them. (Needs **Top 20 of a
  year or month**.)
- **Recently played**: everything played lately, newest first, with runs
  from one album together. **Play again** plays one again.

To forget your history, use **Clear history…** in **Settings ›
Features**.

### Library health

**Library health** lists problems in your files. See
[chapter 12](12-library-health.md#the-library-health-report).

### Now Playing, Visualizer and Queue

These show the music that's playing: the **Now Playing** view (only listed
in the sidebar if **Show Now Playing in the sidebar** is on in **Settings ›
Display**; clicking the player bar's track opens it either way), the
**Visualizer** ([chapter 8](08-visualizations.md)) and the **Queue**
([chapter 3](03-playing-music.md#the-queue)).

## Browsing the library

Under **Library** in the sidebar are **views**: your whole library, grouped
and sorted one way. The ones it starts with are:

| View | Groups by | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **Album artist** | album artist | album, then its tracks |
| **Genre** | genre | album artist, album |
| **Year** | year | album |
| **Folder** | your folders and subfolders, as on disk | |
| **Composer** | composer | work, then its movements (with **Classical works** on) |

A track tagged with two genres ("Rock; Pop") is under both.

Click a group to open it; the location along the top shows where you are,
and **Back** (or Esc) goes up. Each list has:

- **Play** and **Shuffle**, which play everything in it;
- a play button on each group's row, which plays that group;
- **Sort**, where albums are listed: by **Title**, **Year** or **Date
  added**;
- the heart button, **Favourites only**, which keeps only what you've
  hearted (or what is on a hearted album or by a hearted artist). **Show
  everything** turns it off again.

Inside an artist, the artist's name links to their page. Inside an album,
the album's details show above its tracks (see
[Album pages](#album-pages)).

Double-click a track, or select it and press Return, to play its list from
that track: an album plays on from there.

You can change these views, and add your own, in **Settings › Sorting**
([chapter 11](11-settings.md#sorting)). For example: a view of genres, then
years; or tracks by date added. Words like "The" and "A" at the start of a
name are skipped when sorting, so "The Beatles" sorts under B.

### Working with lists

These work in every list of tracks, albums or artists:

- **Select several**: ⌘-click to add one, ⇧-click to add a range, ⇧↑ and
  ⇧↓ to extend the selection, ⌘A to select every row.
- **The menu**: right-click a row, click its **⋯** button, or press ⇧F10.
  The menu acts on everything selected: **Play**, **Play Next**, **Add to
  Queue**, **Add to Playlist**, the heart, **Rating**, **Get Info**, **Go to
  Artist**, **Go to Album**, **Show in Finder**, and **Start Radio**, **More
  Like This** and **Playback Preferences…** when those features are on.
- **Drag** selected rows onto a playlist or the queue in the sidebar, or
  onto the queue panel.
- **Columns**: what track lists show beside the title (artist, album,
  length, plays, rating…) is set in **Settings › Display**.

Tracks of a folder that can't be read right now are dimmed. See
[chapter 12](12-library-health.md).

## Searching

Press ⌘F, or click the **Search** box at the top of the window, and type.
Results appear as you type, grouped into **Artists**, **Albums** and
**Tracks**; **More artists**, **More albums** and **More tracks** show the
rest.

- Words match anywhere in a title or name once they are three letters
  long: "beat" finds "The Beatles" and "Beethoven".
- Narrow a search with a field: `artist:`, `album:`, `title:`, `composer:`
  or `genre:`. Put quotes round names with spaces: `artist:"pink floyd"`.
- Search by year with `year:1994`, or a range with `year:1990-1999`.
- Combine them: `genre:jazz year:1955-1965 blue`.

Click an artist to open their page, an album to open it, or a track to play
its album from that track. ⌘-click and ⇧-click select several tracks for
the menu. Clear the box, or press Esc, to leave the results.

## Artist pages

Open an artist from **Artists**, from the artist's name inside a view, from
**Go to Artist** in a track's menu, or from a search.

<!-- Screenshot: an artist page with a biography and albums (demo library). -->

An artist's page shows:

- **About**: who they are, from MusicBrainz: born or formed, where, and
  when they died or disbanded; and their **Official website**.
- Their **biography**, the start of their Wikipedia article (credited
  under it); **Read more** shows the rest. Turn biographies off with **Show
  album descriptions and artist biographies** in **Settings › Display**.
- **Similar artists**: artists in your library like this one; with
  **Recommendations from outside the library** on, artists you don't have
  too. **More** shows more, **Fewer** fewer. (Needs **Similar artists**.)
- Their albums in your library, grouped as **Albums**, **EPs**, **Singles**,
  **Live albums**, **Compilations**, **Soundtracks** and **Other
  releases**. Click one to open it; double-click to play it.
- **Appears on**: other artists' albums they are on.
- **Releases not in your library**: opens a list of the releases
  MusicBrainz knows for them that you don't have, each linked to its
  MusicBrainz page. **Check again** asks MusicBrainz anew.

The heart beside the name makes the artist a favourite. If the page shows
the wrong person (many artists share a name), click **Wrong artist?**; see
[chapter 6](06-online-information.md#fixing-a-wrong-match).

## Album pages

Open an album by clicking it anywhere: in a view, on Home, on an artist's
page, in a search, or with **Go to Album**.

<!-- Screenshot: an album page with its cover, facts, description and tracks (demo library). -->

Above its tracks, an album shows:

- its **cover**. Click it to choose another
  ([chapter 5](05-track-and-album-details.md#covers));
- a line of facts: release date, label and catalogue number, country,
  format ("2 × CD") and type. Choose which in **Settings › Display**;
- where those facts came from, and whether the album has been matched
  online yet, with **Find Details…** and **Choose Cover…**;
- its **description**, from Wikipedia, when there is one; **Read more**
  shows the rest;
- **Details**: a table of every fact with its source (your tags, or an
  online source). **Hide details** closes it;
- **Works**: for classical albums, each work with its movements under it,
  its composer and conductor, and **Play work** (needs **Classical
  works**);
- **More in** and the genre's name: five other albums sharing a genre with this one,
  picked at random; **Draw again** picks five more (needs **More in this
  genre**);
- **More like this**: albums in your library most like this one, each
  saying why ("from 1972", "on Blue Note", "played together 4 times").
  Click the heading to open it (needs **Recommendations**).

The heart beside the title makes the album a favourite.

## More Like This

**More Like This** in the menu of a track, album or artist (with
**Recommendations** on) opens a list of what in your library is most like
it, each with the reasons it was picked: the same artist or composer,
related artists, the same era, genre or label, similar loudness, and how
often you've played them together. **Play All** plays them; **Add All to
Queue** queues them. For an artist, artists outside your library are listed
too while **Recommendations from outside the library** is on.
